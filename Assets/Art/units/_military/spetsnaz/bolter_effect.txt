According to my Next War Update mod, this unit is the first available futuristic unitclass
 in a game. It's an armed by a primitive plasma-throwing gun our times uniform-wearing soldiers.
Later they just like other firearmed units are getting replaced by more advanced weapons.




BOLTER_FIRE folder is to Effects folder in your CIV4...\art folder

This - is to XML\Misc\CIV4EffectInfos

		<EffectInfo>
			<Type>EFFECT_BOLTER_FIRE</Type>
			<Description>Primitive plasma thrower effect</Description>
			<fScale>1.0</fScale>
			<fUpdateRate>1.0</fUpdateRate>
			<Path>Art/Effects/BOLTER_FIRE/BOLTER_FIRE.nif</Path>
			<bIsProjectile>0</bIsProjectile>
		</EffectInfo>

This - is to XML\Audio\AudioDefines


		<SoundData>
			<SoundID>SND_BOLTER_FIRE</SoundID>
			<Filename>Sounds/Units/BOLTER_FIRE</Filename>
			<LoadType>DYNAMIC_RES</LoadType>
			<bIsCompressed>1</bIsCompressed>
			<bInGeneric>1</bInGeneric>
		</SoundData>


This - is to XML\Audio\Audio3DScripts


	<Script3DSound>
		<ScriptID>AS3D_UN_BOLTER_FIRE</ScriptID>
		<SoundID>SND_BOLTER_FIRE</SoundID>
		<SoundType>GAME_SFX</SoundType>
		<iMinVolume>90</iMinVolume>
		<iMaxVolume>100</iMaxVolume>
		<iPitchChangeDown>0</iPitchChangeDown>
		<iPitchChangeUp>0</iPitchChangeUp>
		<bLooping>0</bLooping>
		<iMinTimeDelay>0</iMinTimeDelay>
		<iMaxTimeDelay>0</iMaxTimeDelay>
		<StartPosition>NONE</StartPosition>
		<EndPosition>NONE</EndPosition>
		<iMinVelocity>0</iMinVelocity>
		<iMaxVelocity>0</iMaxVelocity>
		<iMinDistanceFromListener>0</iMinDistanceFromListener>
		<iMaxDistanceFromListener>0</iMaxDistanceFromListener>
		<iMinDistanceForMaxVolume>500</iMinDistanceForMaxVolume>
		<iMaxDistanceForMaxVolume>1000</iMaxDistanceForMaxVolume>
		<iMinCutoffDistance>7000</iMinCutoffDistance>
		<iMaxCutoffDistance>7000</iMaxCutoffDistance>

		<iLengthOfSound>0</iLengthOfSound>
		<fMinDryLevel>1.0</fMinDryLevel>

bolter_fire.mp3 - is to Sounds\Units  - possibly it's not the best sound, there is no problem to replace it by something more cool.
