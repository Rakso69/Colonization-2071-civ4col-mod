  15.08.2026 Colonization 2071 2.01 Patchnotes by Kaszkaj/Rakso:
  ☑️Restored back the old versions UI which was unique for Colonization 2071.
  ☑️Changed roads and Bridge visual looks to futuristic.
  ☑️Fixed Fortifications to no longer require or replace Fabrication Plant.
  ☑️Arsenal hammers cost decreased by 900→600.
  ☑️Fixed and added missing projectiles during attack/defend animations for many armed units.
  ☑️Changed Pasture visual look to futuristic.
  ☑️Minor improvements fixes like animations, size etc..
  ☑️Implemented space flags visual effect to unit flags.
  ☑️Introducing a new experimental category of units, BOSSES: So far there is only one BOSS-tier unit in the mod available exclusively for the Alien gameplay to fight against. Such units are intended to be challengingly overpowered.
  ☑️Restored back old(new) promotions system, which the mod had in it's oldest versions.
  ☑️Updated the models of many units & buildings & improvements & terrain features and their avatars across the mod.
  ☑️6 New units: Unique Privateer, Unique Freighter, Unique Stormtrooper, Unique Artillery, Unique Cruiser and Unique Convoy.
  ☑️Restored back many map bonuses that got lost over versions updates.
  ☑️Experimental⭐Research Ideas cost decreased by 25%; 100→75 80→60 40→30 30→22 20→15.
  ☑️Changed Plasteel Mill visual looks to futuristic.
  ☑️The Fortifications building was renamed to Domed City. It is intended to grant immunity to radiation effects in the future, when nuclear bombs would get added.
  ☑️Xenotoxins and Phase Desequencers promotions don't do anything for now, because the technical implementation(categorisation) of Feral Units and Cosmic Horrors(Bosses) is not yet in the game.
  ☑️The ancient mound temporarily does not prevent you from building Improvements. In the future. It will be required to excavate it to remove this restriction.
  ☑️Civilizations colours and flags are now locked. You won't receive a random colour when playing some civilization which could not share the same exact one colour with another civilization that had spawned in the given game.
