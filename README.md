Patchnotes:

☑️Introducing a new experimental category of units, BOSSES: So far there is only one BOSS-tier unit in the mod available exclusively for the Alien gameplay to fight against. Such units are intended to be challengingly overpowered.

☑️Updated the models of many units and their avatars across the mod.

☑️6 New units: Unique Privateer, Unique Freighter, Unique Stormtrooper, Unique Artillery, Unique Cruiser and Unique Convoy.

☑️The Lady in Black of the Syndicate Colonies makes it's return.

👽🟩 Alien:

☑️Fixed faction leaders traits granting exclusive genetic traits (global promotions) to their units.

☑️Restored back an old script that allows Aliens to start the game with a Saucer (Corvette).

☑️Adjusted unit sizes. (Alien Mecha, Alien Corsair, Battlestar etc.)

☑️Alien (Replaces Free Colonist) should not override and replace earthlings Free Colonists now.

☑️All Alien Unique space units are now amphibious (can traverse both space and planets landscape) ~This change is exclusive only for the Alien civilization and their space units.

☑️Mutant (Replaces Artillery) is now harder to unlock.

☑️New unique unit for Alien Civs: Carrion. (Replaces Freighter)

☑️New unique unit for Alien Civs: Alien Palanquin. (Replaces Convoy)

☑️Updated the visual looks of Alien Garden (now Alien Fertilizers) and Alien Plantation (now Future Farm).

☑️Aliens can now build a Megastructure (Wonder) ~indicated by the Capital icon over colony when it's built in one.

☑️Fixed the texture of UFO.
